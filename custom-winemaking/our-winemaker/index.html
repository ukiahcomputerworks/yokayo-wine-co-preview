Our winemaker

David Rosenthal brings a career of North Coast winemaking and operational leadership to Yokayo Wine Company.

Before joining Yokayo, David served as Director of Winemaking and General Manager at Redwood Valley Cellars. He also worked as a winemaker at Konocti Vineyards and Buena Vista Winery.

David earned a Bachelor of Science in Fermentation Science from the University of California, Davis in 1979. He began his winemaking career at Konocti Winery, a 28-grower cooperative in Lake County.

At Konocti and later at Buena Vista Winery in Carneros, David made a wide range of varietal wines under the guidance of winemaking legend Andre Tchelistcheff. His experience has produced a deep understanding of the many factors that shape a wine's flavor profile.
